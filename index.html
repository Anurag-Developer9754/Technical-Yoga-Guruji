What are the class timings?

Personal yoga classes run from 5:00 to 8:00 AM, and centre classes from 6:00 to 9:00 PM. Online slots can be arranged — just message us on WhatsApp.

Yes. Online classes are available for students who can’t attend at the centre. Message us on WhatsApp and we’ll confirm the available slots.
